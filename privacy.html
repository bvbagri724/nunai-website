Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are. NUNAI LIMITED (company number 09949092, registered in England and Wales), Nautilus House, Earl Grey Way, North Shields, NE29 6AR, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. Contact: apps@nunai.co.uk.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit nunai.co.uk or contact us. Each of our apps also has its own privacy notice, shown in the app and on its store page, which explains exactly what that app collects and why.

2. Information we collect

  • When you email us: your name, email address and anything you choose to tell us.
  • When you visit this website: this website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.
  • When you use our apps: see the privacy notice for that app.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To answer your questions and provide support – our legitimate interests in running our business, or to take steps you ask for before entering a contract.
  • To keep our website and apps secure – our legitimate interests.
  • To meet our legal duties, for example tax and accounting records – legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run our business (such as our email and hosting providers), and with authorities where the law requires it. Our providers may store data outside the UK; where they do, we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as adequacy regulations or approved contract clauses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep emails and support records for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless we need them longer for a legal reason. Business and tax records are kept for six years, as UK law requires.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to: give you a copy of your information; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; and transfer it to another organisation. Email apps@nunai.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113.

8. Changes

We will update this page if we change how we handle personal information. The date at the top shows the latest version.
